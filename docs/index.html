Questions

Will it buy things on its own?

Never without your explicit okay. Every purchase, message, or post waits for your yes.

Does it work when I’m away from home?

Yes. The phone just sits in a drawer at home, plugged in, and your agent reaches it from wherever you are.

What do I have to do to set it up?

About 10 minutes, once. Your agent walks you through every step. Then plug the phone in, leave it in a drawer, and never touch it again.

Is my stuff private?

Yes. It runs on your own phone at home, not in someone else’s data center. Sign-in codes come from your email and are never stored.

What if the phone restarts?

It comes back on its own. It stays in the drawer, and you never touch it.

Does it cost anything?

No. burner is free and open source, and the one other app it uses, Tailscale, is free for personal use. For a normal setup at home, there’s nothing to pay for.
