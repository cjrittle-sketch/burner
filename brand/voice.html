The voice

Four voice traits

Plain-spoken

Everyday words, short sentences, contractions. If your mum wouldn't say it, rewrite it.

Not dumbed down. Not techy.

Plug the phone in, leave it in a drawer, and never touch it again.

Cheeky, with a wink

Humor comes from real life: streaks, lowballing, the store app that never works. At most one wink per piece.

Not sly about rules. Not snarky at users.

Keep my Snapchat streaks alive while I'm on vacation.

Specific

Name the app, the task, the number. "10 minutes, once" beats "quick setup".

Not vague. Not hype.

Message that Marketplace seller and offer 20 percent less.

Straight about control

Answer the worry first. It's your phone, at home, and nothing happens without your yes.

Not defensive. Not legalese.

Never without your explicit okay. Every purchase, message, or post waits for your yes.

Rules

Do and don't

Do

  • Say "AI assistant" every time. Never "agent", "your agent" or "AI agent".
  • Write "burner" in lowercase, even at the start of a sentence.
  • Name real apps and real chores.
  • Start FAQ answers with Yes, No or the fact.
  • Mention "your yes" wherever money, messages or posts come up.
  • Say it runs on your own phone at home, on your Wi-Fi.
  • Say it "uses your location like any phone would".
  • Use digits for numbers: 10 minutes, 20 percent.
  • Keep the wink to one per piece. The only emoji is 😉.

Don't

  • Explain how it works under the hood on consumer pages.
  • Use terminal, shell or command framing, or code-looking jokes.
  • Say it "shares your location".
  • Promise it's "undetectable" or that it gets past or tricks apps.
  • Play up the spy or crime side of "burner".
  • Use hype: revolutionary, seamless, unlock, supercharge, AI-powered.
  • Stack exclamation marks or use sparkle emoji.
  • Make fun of people who don't get it.
  • Repeat the same reassurance three times on one page.

Rewrites of current copy

Most of useburner.si is already on voice. These are the lines that drift.

Meta description

burner gives your AI agent its own Android phone, so it can use the apps you use.

Rewrite

Give your AI assistant a spare Android phone at home, so it can use the apps you use. Free, no account.

This is what shows in Google results, so it should match the tagline and say "AI assistant".
Under the setup prompt

Your agent reads the guide and handles the rest.

Rewrite

Your AI assistant reads the guide and does the rest.

"Agent" appears three times on the page. Swap all three for "AI assistant".
FAQ: away from home

…and your agent reaches it from wherever you are.

Rewrite

Yes. The phone stays plugged in at home, and your AI assistant can use it from wherever you are.

"Reaches it" hints at networking. "Use it" is what people care about.
Setup and two FAQs

Plug the phone in, leave it in a drawer, and never touch it again. (three times)

Rewrite

Keep the full line in Setup. In the FAQs, say "About 10 minutes, once." and "It comes back on its own."

A great line loses its punch when it repeats. Use it once, where it lands hardest.
README, if quoted anywhere public

A spare phone on your network is a legit client those apps cannot tell apart from you holding it.

Rewrite

It's a real phone on your home Wi-Fi, so to your apps it looks like you on the couch.

Same fact, without "client" or the get-past-the-app tone.
